Joshua Adesegun

Angle Zero is the event marketing practice of Joshua Adesegun.

Joshua has spent three years marketing business events for technology, finance, trade and civic audiences across Africa: as an independent consultant to clients such as Loveworld, and in-house on the growth team at TechCabal and Zikoko. His background spans performance and email marketing, brand strategy, user acquisition and marketing operations across multiple industries, and he holds an MBA in Marketing.

He is the accountable lead on every Angle Zero engagement, from the first brief to the post-event report, working with a vetted network of specialists in campaigns, PR, content, design, web development, registration, onsite operations and analytics.
