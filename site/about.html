About Angle Zero

One accountable lead.

Angle Zero is the marketing side of business events in West Africa. One principal owns every engagement from the first brief to the post-event report, with a vetted network of specialists brought in for each event.

Founder & Principal

Oluwatosin Joshua Adesegun

Joshua has spent three years marketing business events for technology, finance and trade audiences in Nigeria and Ghana, from Moonshot by TechCabal to the Loveworld Trade & Investment Forum. His background spans performance marketing, email and brand strategy, and he holds an MBA in Marketing.

He is the accountable lead on every Angle Zero engagement, from the first brief to the post-event report, working with a vetted network of specialists in PR, design, web development, onsite operations and analytics.

How we work

Three things we won't trade.

01 · One lead

The person who scopes your event reports on it.

No hand-offs between a pitch team and a delivery team. One name on every update, from T-90 to T+14.

02 · Show-ups over sign-ups

We count who walks through the door.

Registrations are easy. We plan, and report, against the people who actually arrive and fit your audience.

03 · Only what's on record

Results you can check.

Figures are shown only where they are on record and cleared for publication, and in-house roles are labelled as such.
