How we work

A countdown, not a guess.

Every engagement runs on fixed checkpoints counted back from event day.

  1. T-90

    Brief

    Objectives, audience criteria, budget and approvals agreed in writing.

  2. T-60

    Launch

    Audience map, channel plan and partners approved. Campaigns go live.

  3. T-30

    Forecast

    On track, at risk or off track, with a recovery plan if needed.

  4. T-7

    Attendance drive

    Confirmations, reminders and the show-up programme.

  5. T-0

    Event day

    Onsite registration, data capture and live coverage.

  6. T+14

    Report

    Attendance, audience quality and next-edition recommendations.

What we measure.

01

Show-up rate.

02

Audience match.
